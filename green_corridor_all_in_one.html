Smartphone-as-a-Sensor (SaaS) • 108 Driver App vs Legacy Dedicated Hardware

Instead of installing costly GPS tracker boxes (SIM808/NEO-6M) into every ambulance vehicle, the driver or EMT operates an official Karnataka 108 Arogya Kavacha Mobile Application. Telemetry flows via 4G/5G directly into the Mysuru Central Traffic Police (MCTP) Command Cloud.

01 • AUTHENTICATION

Driver Login & Dispatch ID

Driver enters Employee ID (DRV-MYS-108) and 108 Incident Ticket ID. Eliminates misuse by unauthorized drivers speeding without patients.

02 • TRIAGE SELECTION

Medical Urgency Tier

Paramedic chooses Triage level: P1 Code Red (Trauma/Cardiac → Cascading Green Wave) vs P3 Blue (Routine → Standard traffic).

03 • CLOUD BROKER

MCTP Police Control Room

Central server monitors city-wide map and issues low-latency preemption overrides to roadside signal microcontrollers.

04 • ZERO EXTRA COST

₹0 Vehicle Hardware Overhead

Utilizes phone’s internal dual-frequency GPS + NavIC satellite receivers. Zero installation downtime, zero vehicle battery drain.

📚 IEEE Base Papers, Official DOIs & Academic Literature Survey

Official peer-reviewed IEEE Transactions & IEEE Access research papers underpinning our autonomous Green Corridor architecture, cascading multi-junction signal preemption, dynamic GLOSA speed advisory, and collision-free Petri net safety invariants.

IEEE TRANSACTIONS ON ITS IEEE ACCESS • C-ITS
[PRIMARY BASE PAPER 1: CASCADING GREEN WAVE] Vol. 21, Issue 7 • July 2020

On-Demand Greenwave for Emergency Vehicles in a Time-Varying Road Network With Uncertainties

Authors: Wang Min, Long Yu, Peng Chen, Meng Zhang, Yong Liu, and Jia Wang

Focus: Formulates a dynamic mathematical model for an on-demand green wave tailored for emergency vehicles traversing multi-junction arterials. Employs stochastic delay compensation and advance queue clearance to eliminate emergency stoppage.

Citation: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, vol. 21, no. 7, pp. 3056–3068, 2020.
Project Mapping: Mathematical basis for our 3-junction cascading green wave along Irwin Road (J1 → J2 → J3), delivering up to 155s patient delay reduction.
📄 View on IEEE Xplore ↗ 🔗 DOI: 10.1109/TITS.2019.2923802 ↗
[PRIMARY BASE PAPER 2: IOT & CONTROLLER] Vol. 6 • Nov. 2018

EVP-STC: Emergency Vehicle Priority and Self-Organising Traffic Control at Intersections Using Internet-of-Things Platform

Authors: Ajmal Khan, Farman Ullah, Zeeshan Kaleem, Saeed Ur Rahman, Haseeb Anwar, and You-Ze Cho

Focus: Proposes a distributed IoT architecture where emergency vehicles transmit continuous GPS telemetry to road-segment intersection controllers, autonomously overriding fixed cyclic signals and optimizing corridor passage.

Citation: IEEE Access, vol. 6, pp. 68242–68254, 2018. doi: 10.1109/ACCESS.2018.2879644.
Project Mapping: Powers our ESP32 GPS Tracker unit (`esp32_emergency_gps_tracker.ino`), 500m geofence engine (`backend/engine.py`), and real-time WebSocket state streaming (`backend/main.py`).
📄 View on IEEE Xplore ↗ 🔗 DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2018.2879644 ↗
[BASE PAPER 3: GLOSA SPEED ADVISORY] Vol. 24, Issue 6 • June 2023

Assessing Connected Vehicle's Response to Green Light Optimal Speed Advisory From Field Operational Test and Scaling Up

Authors: Aleksandar Stevanovic, Milan Zlatkovic, and Cameron Kergaye

Focus: Evaluates Connected Vehicle (CV) driver compliance with Green Light Optimal Speed Advisory (GLOSA) systems, analyzing how dynamic speed advice eliminates abrupt deceleration and maximizes corridor throughput.

Citation: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, vol. 24, no. 6, pp. 6051–6063, 2023.
Project Mapping: Directly implemented as our live in-cabin GLOSA Speed Dial (50–65 km/h) and Web Speech audio navigation alerts (`frontend/driver_app.html`).
📄 View on IEEE Xplore ↗ 🔗 DOI: 10.1109/TITS.2022.3187532 ↗
[BASE PAPER 4: COLLISION SAFETY & PETRI NETS] Vol. 16, Issue 4 • Aug. 2015

Design of Traffic Safety Control Systems for Emergency Vehicle Preemption Using Timed Petri Nets

Authors: Yuh-Sheng Huang, Yuan-Sun Weng, and MengChu Zhou

Focus: Develops formal deadlock-free, collision-free safety controllers for emergency preemption using Timed Petri Nets, proving that strict all-red clearance intervals prevent traffic conflicts during abrupt phase transitions.

Citation: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, vol. 16, no. 4, pp. 2113–2120, 2015.
Project Mapping: Mathematical foundation for our strict 1-arm-green mutex, 3.0s yellow safety clearance buffer, and 75s watchdog fail-safe timeout in `backend/engine.py`.
📄 View on IEEE Xplore ↗ 🔗 DOI: 10.1109/TITS.2015.2395419 ↗
[BASE PAPER 5: V2X & CIVILIAN LANE YIELDING] Vol. 25, Issue 7 • July 2024 (Latest Benchmark)

V2X and Deep Reinforcement Learning-Aided Mobility-Aware Lane Changing for Emergency Vehicle Preemption in Connected Autonomous Transport Systems

Authors: Chen Ding, Ivan Wang-Hei Ho, Edward Chung, and Tingting Fan

Focus: Investigates V2X communication to orchestrate cooperative civilian lane changes ahead of approaching emergency vehicles, demonstrating that advance civilian yielding to outer lanes dramatically reduces emergency response times.

Citation: IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, vol. 25, no. 7, pp. 7281–7293, July 2024.
Project Mapping: Direct inspiration for our civilian vehicle yielding physics (`updateCorridorPhysics`), where civilian cars yield to the outer left kerbside lane upon detecting the emergency vehicle within 320m.
📄 View on IEEE Xplore ↗ 🔗 DOI: 10.1109/TITS.2024.3350334 ↗
